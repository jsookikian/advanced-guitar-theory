# Context: how this lesson was made

Built 24 Nov 2026 from three planning documents (core ideas of Jordan's video, a lesson plan and a lesson-type proposal) drafted by another tool from the video's captions. Jordan asked for them to be checked independently, then redirected the lesson twice.

## What was checked

- Triad plan (dropped): Asus4 = Dsus2 and Dsus4 = Gsus2, Am7 and D7 diatonic to A Dorian, minor pentatonics inside A Dorian, and a brute-force search returned exactly the plan's 14 positions. "G/A is A9sus4" was wrong: A G B D has no 5th.
- Drop 2 7sus4 plan (built): both grips Jordan named are real Drop 2 voicings (`xx5535` = G C D A from the close chord C D G A, D7sus4 with the 4th in the bass; `xx5755` = G D E A, A7sus4 with the ♭7 in the bass). Between them G and A stay and C and D each go up a whole step. In A Dorian a 7sus4 works on A, B, D and E. `xx5535` over A is Am7(add11) without the 5th (also no 9th), not "Am11".

## Why the lesson is shaped this way

- The triad plan (Asus4, Dsus4, G over A with a doubled note) was not Drop 2 and Jordan called one of its voicings nonsense, so it was replaced with Drop 2 voicings of 7sus4 chords.
- Jordan then asked for the arc: build the sus voicings from a Drop 2 dominant 7 (3rd to 4th), all four inversions on the top strings first, then the middle and low string sets, then use both A7sus4 and D7sus4 over A Dorian. The closed-to-Drop-2 derivation was removed (the Drop 2 lesson covers it). The grey caption row under the card text was removed too, except on the Vamp steps where it names the loop chord.

## Judgement calls

- The four inversions climb the neck (1st, 2nd, 3rd, then root position an octave up) so the board moves up smoothly; each is the lowest voicing that also fits as a dominant 7 without an open string.
- The Strings step shows root position on the middle and low sets only, then points to Explore for the other inversions.
- The Pair and Walk steps come after Make and Strings, so the learner knows the voicings before seeing them as a pair.
- The drone is the key's root and is a Settings toggle, not part of the board.
- The video's own loop ("C to F") and the G sharp minor pentatonic remark are unconfirmed and not taught. The lesson stays in A Dorian over Am7 to D7. The fingerings are our own, not read off the video.
